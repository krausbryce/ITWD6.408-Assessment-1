Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020 of New Zealand.

Collection of Information

Personal information is collected only when it is necessary for a lawful purpose connected with our functions or activities. Information may be collected directly from you or, where permitted by law, from other sources.

Use of Information

Any personal information collected will be used only for the purpose for which it was obtained, or for purposes that are directly related to that purpose, unless otherwise permitted by law.

Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to personal information is restricted to authorized individuals.

Disclosure of Information

Personal information will not be disclosed to third parties except where required by law, authorized by the individual concerned, or otherwise permitted under the Privacy Act 2020.

Access and Correction

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to request correction of that information if you believe it is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date.

Retention of Information

Personal information is retained only for as long as it is required for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law.

Cookies and Website Analytics

This website may use cookies and analytics technologies to improve user experience, monitor website performance, and understand how visitors use our services. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although some features of the website may not function correctly.

Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, individuals have rights regarding how their personal information is collected, used, stored, and disclosed, including the right to access and request correction of their information.

This Privacy Policy is intended to align with the Information Privacy Principles contained within the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.
